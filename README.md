## 📊 Overview
**Shoh Ake Bank Strategy v1 Pro** is a high-precision, institutional-grade TradingView indicator built on **Smart Money Concepts (SMC)**, bank manipulation models, and liquidity sweeps. Designed for scalpers and algorithmic traders, it automates liquidity hunt detection, precise risk management (ATR & Razgon pips), and structured multi-target Take Profits.

---

## 🌟 Key Features
* **🏦 Bank Manipulation & Liquidity Sweeps:** Automatically detects high and low sweeps where institutional banks grab liquidity before major reversals.
* **⚡ Displacement Filter:** Filters out weak fakeouts by requiring strong impulsive volume candles for entry confirmation.
* **🛡️ Dual Risk Management Modes:**
  * *ATR-Based:* Dynamic stop loss powered by Average True Range (Multiplier: 1.5x).
  * *Fixed Pips (Razgon):* Fixed tight stop loss (e.g., 5-10 pips) for aggressive scalping.
* **🎯 Multi-Target Take Profit (RR 1:1, 1:2, 1:3):** Built-in risk-to-reward structuring with visual boxes and live pip counters.
* **📈 Real-Time Dashboard:** A sleek built-in metrics table displaying active SL mode, current trend (EMA 200), active trading sessions (London/NY/Asia), and liquidity status.
* **🔔 Smart Alerts:** Instant webhook and browser alerts for BUY, SELL, and Liquidity Sweep events.

## 📥 How to Install on TradingView
1. Open [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/).
2. Open the **Pine Editor** at the bottom of the screen.
3. Paste the code from `shoh_ake_strategy.pine`.
4. Click **"Add to Chart"** and configure your settings!
